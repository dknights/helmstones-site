## FAQ
- **Eyebrow:** GOOD TO KNOW
- **Title:** A few straight answers.
- **Body:** Need anything else? Send us a note and we will be happy to help.
- **Question 1:** Are all three watches really £285?
- **Answer 1:** Yes. The Horizon, Sunset and Pride are each priced at £285, with a two-year warranty included.
- **Question 2:** Which movement does each watch use?
- **Answer 2:** The Horizon uses an NH35 automatic movement. The Sunset and Pride use the VH31 quartz movement, chosen for its smooth sweeping seconds hand.
- **Question 3:** Can I swim in a Helmstones watch?
- **Answer 3:** Every model is rated to 100 metres water resistance. Always make sure the crown is fully secured before the watch meets water.
- **Question 4:** How do I check availability?
- **Answer 4:** Use any enquiry link on this page or email us directly. We will reply with the latest release and availability details.
